## Decisions and context

User explicitly requested deep review after each delivery while urgent loop-supervision delivery was already running. This is an approved dependent follow-up; do not mutate that active task graph or expected branch HEAD. Implement against the completed supervisor interfaces, reusing its cursor/outbox, ownership and policy primitives; no second scheduler or daemon.

The current core binary has batuta review --base <ref> --spec <path> --full --out <directory>, not a literal --deep flag. The batuta-review skill's deep mode selects the engine. Probe actual capabilities and retain exact original base and delivered spec; archived plan paths and spec digests must resolve correctly. Deep review of a completed delivery should cover its entire change, not silently reuse coverage from another commit/spec. Use an isolated snapshot when needed so the next delivery cannot mutate reviewed source. Missing engine or unresolved snapshot is a visible pending/failed review, never a skipped success or automatic installation.

Run once per unique completed delivery identity as a logical job with durable state and bounded recovery; do not claim exactly-once external execution across a crash without evidence. Persist launch intent and reconcile uncertain reviewer process state before relaunch, bind ownership to this review job, and avoid interference with loop ownership. Production uses routed readonly reviewer adapters and the engine's existing independent proof checks. Tests use fake process/engine results and deterministic clocks; no paid calls or external notifications.

Review report is evidence, not self-approval. Persist it and route findings for conductor judgment through the supervisor's explicit authorization policy. Accepted in-scope corrections can form a bounded correction delivery, followed by review of the new commit. Scope expansion, missing authority and unresolved execution still require a decision. Prevent recursive endless review/fix loops with a chain budget and explicit exhausted state. SHIP does not authorize merge, release, publishing or global installation. Notify locally with status and artifact references; do not claim a spontaneous chat wake-up.

Implementation base: supervisor candidate252ff12 in isolated feat/supervision-review. Supervisor and ACP review engines are running on their own stable branches. Do not change those source trees. The existing supervisor exposes loop --supervise, cursor and file/desktop sinks; its current policy records a bound answer but does not invoke a conductor model or resume the runner. Do not assume those capabilities exist. This plan owns the deep-review job and bounded correction proposals, not a blanket automatic resume policy. Current legacy beta21 journals may carry completed pending_ref_deletions lists; that compatibility issue is being reviewed separately. Never bypass recovery checks just to trigger review.

Responsibility split: task1 implements scheduling and engine execution against immutable snapshots; task2 adds outcome handling and bounded follow-up proposals. Shared context describes the combined outcome and does not require task1 to implement files assigned only to task2. Existing authenticated subscriptions and route are preserved; no automatic installs or paid fallback. Worker sandbox cache/preflight restrictions are known baseline environment limitations, not production bugs. Use temporary writable GOCACHE and report local full-suite limits; independent gates run outside sandbox. Expected red tests or different newly exposed causes do not trigger the same-cause two-failure stop.

Unknown final identity in old journals must remain explicit rather than using a moving branch HEAD. Verify recorded identity matches final delivered commit and remains stable after later branch commits. No change to permission, cleanup or merge policy.
